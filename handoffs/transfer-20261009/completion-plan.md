## Two coverage directions must remain separate

`CompilerCertificateTransport.Coverage` proves selected local rows occur in full rows. It supports soundness because a full satisfying assignment satisfies those local constraints. Completeness additionally needs a kernel proof that the construction covers and satisfies **every** full row, including link/outlined rows and padding; preferably use an indexed exact row-origin partition. Python's full-stream comparator supplies data to that proof, not the proof itself.

`SourceGraphEvaluation.satisfies_iff_assertions` deliberately leaves assertion truth open. The evaluator computes node values, while owned algorithm lemmas must establish the assertions. Graph equality also does not by itself establish `TransferSem`. Keep the graph/algorithm bridge and row/compiler bridge independently visible.
